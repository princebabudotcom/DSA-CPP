Not the 20-problem quick set, and not a random 450-question dump either. This is a **complete but curated** list — every problem here was chosen because it's either (a) the canonical example of a pattern, or (b) a level-up variant of that pattern you'll actually be asked in interviews. Organized by topic in the order you should learn them — each topic builds on the last.

Check off each row. If a problem takes >1 attempt, star it — that's your re-revision list.

**Total: 150 problems** — matches what most SDE-1 interview loops actually draw from. A few LC problems above are marked **(premium)**: they're LeetCode-Premium/paywalled, but they're standard enough that GFG or the underlying pattern (union-find, BFS-on-grid, DAG topo-sort) covers them just as well if you don't have Premium.

**Suggested pace:** ~3-4 topics/week alongside your existing schedule gets you through this in ~5-6 weeks. Prioritize sections 1-7 (Arrays through Trees) first — they cover the bulk of what gets asked, and later sections (DP, Graphs) build directly on those foundations.
